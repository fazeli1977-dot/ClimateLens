1. Project Title:
ClimateLens: High-Resolution Climate Risk & Anomaly Intelligence

2. High-Level Summary (Short Summary):
ClimateLens is an interactive climate risk assessment engine that leverages NASA Earth Observation indices (NASA POWER, GPM, MODIS/VIIRS) to compute localized heat stress, thermal volatility, and precipitation anomalies. Built with FastAPI and a modern geospatial dashboard, it provides real-time multi-hazard vulnerability scoring and 7-day adaptive advisories for urban planners and frontline communities.

3. How Did You Address This Challenge?:
We developed an end-to-end analytical platform that transforms raw satellite meteorological data into actionable risk metrics. ClimateLens features a dynamic geospatial coordinate picker, calculates biometeorological stress (Heat Index), measures microclimate volatility over 30-day moving windows, and projects 7-day anomaly trajectories with automated civil preparedness alerts (Low, Moderate, High, Critical).

4. How Did You Use NASA Earth Science Data?:
Our multi-parameter engine integrates concepts and indicators inspired by NASA Earth observation missions:
- Surface Atmospheric Data: Benchmarked against NASA POWER parameters (T2M, RH2M) for surface thermal and moisture profiling.
- Precipitation Dynamics: Modeled after GPM (Global Precipitation Measurement) anomaly regimes to gauge drought and deluge vectors.
- Land Surface Radiation: Grounded in MODIS/VIIRS thermal anomaly and surface temperature methodologies for composite risk scoring.

5. Space Agency Data & Open-Source Resources:
- NASA POWER API (Prediction of Worldwide Energy Resources)
- NASA GPM (Global Precipitation Measurement)
- NASA Terra/Aqua MODIS & Suomi NPP VIIRS (LST & Thermal Anomalies)
- Open-Meteo Historical & Forecast Weather API
